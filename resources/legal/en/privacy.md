- **Account data:** name, email address, optional phone number, role, department, profile image if you add one, and your password (stored only as a hash).
- **Content:** what you and your colleagues enter into the Service, such as projects, tasks, comments, mentions and time entries.
- **Company and billing data:** company name, billing email address, plan, number of seats, and subscription status. Payments are handled by our payment provider, and we do not store card numbers.
- **Usage and technical data:** sign-in times, IP address and browser information in security and activity logs, and error logs.
- **Communications:** messages you send us, and the invitation, verification and billing emails we send you.
